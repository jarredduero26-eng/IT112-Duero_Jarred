SkyShield AI

An Intelligent Weather Risk Prediction and Personalized Preparedness System for Students

Heavy rain, thunderstorms and extreme heat can affect students' safety, attendance and travel. General forecasts do not always tell a student what to prepare. SkyShield AI turns weather conditions into clear risk levels and personal recommendations.

  1. Analyze weather data to identify rain, thunderstorm and heat risks.
  2. Classify risks as low, moderate or high.
  3. Give personalized alerts and preparation tips.
  4. Improve students' awareness before travelling to school.
  5. Evaluate functionality and usability.

  • Weather information input
  • AI-assisted risk scoring
  • Low / Moderate / High classification
  • Personalized preparedness recommendations
  • On-screen alerts for severe conditions
  • Student-friendly interface with accounts and history

  • This offline version uses the weather values you enter; it does not download live forecasts.
  • Results depend on the accuracy of your inputs; weather can change unexpectedly.
  • Accounts live only in the browser you use.
  • Not a replacement for official warnings or emergency instructions.
